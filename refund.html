Refund Policy

Last Updated: May 31, 2026

1. Supportive Re-balancing

We recognize that slow journeys require absolute peace of mind. If your travel plans change due to wellness concerns, family events, or flight disruptions, we strive to assist in rescheduling your stay or retreat calendar.

2. Direct Cancellation Tiers

  • 14+ Days Notice: 100% complete refund processed instantly to the original payment channel.
  • 7 - 14 Days Notice: 50% cash refund and 50% Peepal Tree Circle credit voucher redeemable on any upcoming experiences.
  • Under 7 Days Notice: Credit voucher for 50% of booking value (unless rescheduled under extreme medical certification).

3. Processing Timeline

Refund requests submitted directly via the customer profile or Vythiri support channel are verified and credited within 5 working business days.
