Frequently Answered Questions

Clear answers regarding bookings, retreats, facilitator registry, and cancellation rules.

Experiences & Stays

Is Peepal Tree a traditional booking OTA or hotel aggregator?

No. We are a closed-circle hospitality and experiences network. We focus on connecting travelers directly to verified local educators (facilitators) and boutique properties who host courses together. Stays are co-allocated to serve specific experiences or retreat timelines.

What is the difference between an Experience, a Retreat, and a Gathering?

Experiences are short, seat-based single sessions (3 to 6 hours) focusing on breathwork, clay architecture, or cooking. Retreats are fully integrated multi-day packages (4 to 5 days) including boutique cabin stays, Sattvic dietary plans, and curated facilitator courses. Gatherings are free or low-cost regional offline meetups designed to build local community connection.

Payments & Cancellations

How does the waitlist approval workflow operate?

Because our retreats and experiences maintain strict attendee limits (between 6 and 15 people max) to guarantee high-fidelity learning, bookings go into a pending queue. Once verified by the facilitator, you will receive an approval link to secure your ticket. If a session is fully booked, you can join the silent waitlist with zero charge.

What is your refund policy for conscious retreats?

We respect the financial schedule of our independent property partners. Cancellations placed 14 days before a retreat start receive a 100% refund. Cancellations made between 7 and 14 days receive a 50% credit. Refunds are completely processed within 5 business days.
