Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 31, 2026

1. Safe Information Practices

At Peepal Tree, we believe digital presence should replicate physical safety. We collect only critical contact information (name, email) necessary to process registrations, waitlist spots, and secure retreat bookings.

2. No Commercial Scraping

We do not lease, trade, or distribute your email records to any third-party tracking corporations. Your coordinates are used strictly by our Vythiri coordinators and the specific facilitators hosting your booked retreats.

3. Security of Data

Any membership billing coordinates are securely locked under Supabase Row Level Security protocols. Audit logs are actively tracked to protect the ecosystem against external breaches.
